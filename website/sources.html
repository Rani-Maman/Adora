Sources

Statistics shown on the homepage banner are cited from published industry research — not estimates by Adora.

Mastercard · Juniper Research (Jan 24, 2024)

Ecommerce fraud trends and statistics merchants need to know — Mastercard News & Trends.

  • $343B+ (banner stat 1): Juniper Research projects cumulative losses to online payment fraud globally between 2023 and 2027 will exceed $343 billion.
  • $48B+ (banner stat 2): Juniper Research estimates the total cost of ecommerce fraud to merchants will exceed $48 billion globally in a single year.

BBC · Fakespot (Dec 2020)

Shopify stores riddled with fakes and fraudsters — BBC News, reporting Fakespot analysis of 124,000 Shopify stores.

  • ~21% (banner stat 3): Nearly 21% of Shopify stores analyzed were linked to fraudulent practices (~26,000 of 124,000). Fakespot breakdown includes problematic sellers (~39% of flagged), possible scam stores (~28%), negative consumer reports, and stores with no transaction history.
